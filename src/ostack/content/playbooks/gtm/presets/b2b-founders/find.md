Start with `o11.people.search`: `seniority` `owner`, the user's `company_headcount` band, and `funding_stage` `seed` or `pre_seed` for recent rounds. For founders posting about a problem the user solves, run `o11.leads.from_topic` on that topic.

Other good places to look: recent seed and pre-seed announcements, Product Hunt and Show HN launches, accelerator demo days, founder posts about hiring their first salespeople, and niche directories for the user's market.
