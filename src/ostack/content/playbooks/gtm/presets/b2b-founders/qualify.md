Typical rules for this use case (the user's own qualify file sharpens them):
- The company builds and sells its own product to businesses, not consumers.
- The contact currently holds a founder or CEO role (check `o11.linkedin.user.profile` or the team page, not an old article).
- Headcount fits the user's range; use `o11.companies.enrich`, LinkedIn or the team page, and treat an unknown size as unproven.
- Exclude agencies, consultancies and dev shops, companies without a live product, and existing customers.
