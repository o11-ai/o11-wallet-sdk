used_by: [ugc]
changed: 0.11.0
---
Follow the UGC skill's experiments reference. Change one thing per experiment (the model, the person, the voice or the hook) and hold everything else fixed. Run it on the cheapest tier that can show the difference: stills before clips, 360p or three seconds before a full take. Put the options in one labelled sheet with `node scripts/match.mjs sheet`, state what each cost and what the full version will cost, and stop for the user's choice. Record the choice and the options not taken in `shoot-plan.md`. The team's standing choices (a presenter, a voice, a preferred model) live in `launch-videos/character.md` and are not asked again.
