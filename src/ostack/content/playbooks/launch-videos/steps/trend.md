Use the `ostack-video-trending` skill. Turn the niche into search phrases, quote, and search until 50 to 150 videos from the last 30 days are kept, saying the price before paging. When the user names competitors, add their active ads from the ad libraries as a separate table. Rank by views per day with `o11 ostack launch-videos trending rank`. Sort the strongest rows by kind (text over footage, talking hook then product, presenter over a screen, voiceover, reaction, skit) and by whether this user's product can honestly be shown that way. Get transcripts for the 10 to 15 strongest and write `trending/hooks.md`: views, first spoken line, first on-screen text, the second the product is first named, who is on camera, format, and the sound with how many kept videos share it. Name the dominant pattern in one line. A pattern needs three videos from three accounts, and its why comes from the top videos' comments, marked inferred. Show the strongest fifteen, then five to eight presenter and voice candidates described in words. Stop for the user's pick: which video or pattern to build on, and whose look and delivery to describe. Import the picks as references; a reference holds facts about a public video and gives no right to its person, voice or footage. Skip this step when the user arrives with a reference or a finished script.
