Use the UGC skill’s recreate reference, then its reference-remake reference. Save acquisition provenance, contact-sheet frames, word timings and a timed beat sheet separately from the new script. Record which appearance, motion, composition, audio or editing properties the user wants to reuse. A request for the same style does not imply the source speaker’s identity, exact words, branding or soundtrack. If acquisition is partial, state what could not be inspected and plan around the available evidence. Break the reference down with the `ostack-video-teardown` skill: every beat gets a `look` (where the lens is, where the subject sits as fractions of the frame, what is in the foreground, the light, the gaze, when the text appears), and the reference keeps its sound's id and link.
