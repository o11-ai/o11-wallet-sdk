Agree the brief before touching footage or code. Start from the video's brief (`o11 ostack launch-videos video <id>`), and ask only what it and the brand kit (`launch-videos/brand.md`, `script.md`, `deliver.md`) leave open. This video's answers belong in its brief in o11, never in the brand kit: save them with `o11 ostack launch-videos update <id> --goal …` (and `--audience`, `--material`, `--storyboard`, `--notes`) so the team sees them on the video's page.

Write `brief.md` in the video folder:
- **What launches:** one sentence.
- **Who watches:** the audience and where it is posted (size from `deliver.md`; length from the brief, else the format's usual length there).
- **The one takeaway:** what someone should remember. One video, one idea.
- **Proof:** real numbers, customers or results the user can stand behind. Never invent them.
- **Material:** the clips (irl) or the screens and the flow to show (product-demo).
- **End card:** the name and the call to action from `brand.md`.
- **Budget:** what this video may spend on wallet tools, from `sound.md`.

Read it back in three lines and get a yes.
