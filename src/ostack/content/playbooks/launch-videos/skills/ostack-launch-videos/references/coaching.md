# Coaching the team's videos

Point out what will cost views while you work, in every review and in every report, without being asked. How to say it, and what you may change yourself versus what needs their yes, is in the ostack-setup skill's `references/first-run.md` ("Coach without being asked"). The standards are [storytelling.md](storytelling.md), [reels.md](reels.md) and [audio.md](audio.md).

## Red flags in a script, cut or reference

Name at most three, worst first, each with the timestamp or line, why it costs viewers and the fix.

1. **A slow hook.** Nothing worth staying for in the first three seconds: a logo, a greeting, "Hi, I'm…", or the context before the point.
2. **More than one idea.** The video tries to say several things, so none lands. One idea per video.
3. **Too long for where it goes.** A reel over about 45 seconds, or a launch film that keeps going after the point is made.
4. **Features, not outcomes.** Screens and feature names without what the viewer gets from them.
5. **No clear ending.** No call to action, or one that does not match the brand kit's last line.
6. **Hard to watch on a phone.** Captions missing, too small or covered by the platform's buttons; text on screen too briefly to read; audio too quiet or music over the voice.
7. **Claims the brand kit does not back.** Numbers, customers or comparisons with no source.

A video with none of these: say it is ready in one line.

## Their script versus yours

- Scripts, cuts and captions you make: fix the flags before you render, and say so in one line.
- Their own script, footage choices or a reference they love: show a tighter version next to theirs (a new first line, a cut, a shorter script) and ask for a one-word yes, or make both when the format makes a second version cheap.

## After results come in

When the team shares how a posted video did (views, watch time, saves), or the trending skill measured it, add a short "What to try next" note: keep what held viewers, change the first three seconds of what lost them, and suggest one variant to test. Never spend on new generation to test without their spending yes.
