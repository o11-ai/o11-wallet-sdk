# Storytelling

Everything on screen is timed to the words: transcribe or generate the voice with word timings, paraphrase each idea
into a headline, and show the idea while it is said.

## Filmed (irl)

1. Contact-sheet every clip and note where each person stands for the whole clip: that decides the open side for glass
   and the free corner for the mark.
2. Read the transcript (`edit/speech.js`) and mark each idea: the problem, what it is, how it works, proof, the ask.
3. One headline per idea, spanning the phrase and holding at least 2.2s. Merge quick neighbours.
4. One glass scene per idea on single-speaker clips, entering on the word that introduces it and leaving on the word
   that changes it.

Example, for "we built a wallet with 3,000 tools your agent can use from day one":

| Spoken | Headline | Glass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "a wallet with 3,000 tools" | `3,000 tools / in one wallet` | the real card, logo tiles gathering, a count to 3,000 |
| "from the moment they onboard" | `Ready the moment / your agent onboards` | agent logos and a switch flipping on |

## Product demo

A demo shows one job done from start to finish, fast, with the real product:

1. **The moment of need** (one line): who has the problem, in their words.
2. **The job, done:** three to five screens, each one action and its result. The camera goes to what changed.
3. **The proof:** the result in numbers, or the before and after.
4. **The end card.**

Narration says what the viewer gets ("every invoice lands in one inbox"), never which button to press. One narration
line per camera rest. Cut anything that is setup, loading or settings unless it is the point.

## Copy

- The user's own words wherever they gave them; their product name exactly as written.
- Real numbers only, consistent across the video. Demo names are demo names, and you say so at handover.
- The call to action is the one line from `launch-videos/brand.md`.
