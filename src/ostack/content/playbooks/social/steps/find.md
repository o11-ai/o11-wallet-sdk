Search each platform with the terms in `social/niche.md` for both top and latest posts inside its freshness window
(defaults: X 24 hours, up to 3 days for big posts; LinkedIn 3 days, up to a week; Instagram 2 weeks). On a strong niche
post, also look at who replied: those people are usually in the niche too.

Keep people, not brands: a founder, operator or practitioner posting their own take counts; a company page, ad, or tool
announcement with no personal take does not. Read the whole post before ranking it: the full text, every slide, the
linked article, and for video what is said and shown on screen (let it play and look at the start, middle and end).

Rank every post you read:
- **Best:** squarely in the niche, by a real person, fresh, with a real take and some traction (on X, about 20 likes or
  more). These get the follows, likes, comments, reposts and quotes first.
- **Close:** by a real person, in or right next to the niche, but weaker on one or two counts: a small account or few
  likes, older than the window but inside its upper bound, a neighboring topic, a short or common take, or a good reply
  under a best post. These keep the follows and likes going once the best posts are used up.
- **Skip:** anything in the niche file's skip list, brands, ads, bots, engagement bait, and off-niche posts. Never
  touched, whatever the count.

Each round, work the best posts found so far, then fill the round with close posts. Running out of best posts is not a
reason to slow down: keep collecting close posts until every target is met. To find more of both, widen in this order
and keep going:
1. Search latest again for posts published since the last search, and drop the like filter to find smaller close posts.
2. Open the replies and quote posts under the strongest niche posts of the day; act on the good replies and their authors.
3. Look at the recent posts of people followed today and of the accounts the niche file names, and at who replied to them.
4. Stretch each platform's freshness window to its upper bound.
5. Search terms close to the niche file's (related roles, topics and phrasings). Name them in the report so the team can
   add the good ones to `social/niche.md`.

Only if a full pass of all five turns up no new best or close post, wait 20 to 30 minutes and start again from the
first. Widening never loosens the skip list or the people-not-brands rule.
