Work the platforms in rotating rounds (a small batch on X, then LinkedIn, then Instagram, then back), so each rests
while the others are worked. In each batch, start with the best posts from the find step, then fill the batch with
close posts:
- **Best posts:** follow the person, like the post, and comment where there is something specific to add. On X, also
  repost the best few posts of the day and quote one only with a short take worth adding.
- **Close posts:** follow the person when their profile is in or next to the niche, and like the post. Comment only
  when there is still something specific to say; most close posts get no comment. No reposts or quotes.

Skip anyone already followed and posts already liked or commented on. Save the day's comments, reposts and quotes for
best posts; follows and likes are what close posts are for, so keep working them after the best posts run out.

Pace: mix action types, never more than 10 of one action in a row, wait a random 10 to 60 seconds between actions on a
platform, and keep under the hourly ceilings and daily ranges in `social/limits.md`. At the start of the run pick
today's number for each cell at random from its range, so days differ, and print them. Keep working each platform
until every target is met, it is stopped, or the day ends, spreading what is left over the hours left.

After every action, confirm it visibly stuck before counting it. At any warning, rate limit, "try again later",
security check, or action that does not stick, stop that platform for the day and note it. Never retry it the same day.

Comments follow `social/comments.md` and the person's `social/voice.md`, and the comments reference in the skill.
