# Pacing

The team's `social/limits.md` wins. Without it, use these ranges and pick today's number for each cell at random at the
start of the run, so the totals differ every day:

| | Follows | Likes | Comments | Reposts | Quotes |
|-|-|-|-|-|-|
| X | 330-350 | 330-350 | 25-30 | 4-5 | 1-2 |
| LinkedIn | 45-50 | 70-80 | 17-20 | none | none |
| Instagram | 90-100 | 133-143 | 8-10 | none | none |

LinkedIn rows apply only for a person who turned LinkedIn on (`LinkedIn: on` in their accounts file).

Hourly ceilings per platform:

| | Follows/hour | Likes/hour | Comments/hour |
|-|-|-|-|
| X | 150 | 150 | 18 |
| LinkedIn | 7 | 10 | 3 |
| Instagram | 40 | 50 | 6 |

An account under about three months old or with few followers starts at half these numbers for its first two weeks.

Spread actions across the whole run and never burst: rotate platforms in rounds, mix action types, never more than 10 of
one action in a row, and wait a random 10 to 60 seconds between actions on the same platform. A full day takes 6 hours
or more, mostly on X.

Aim to reach every target by the end of the day: 23:00 in the person's timezone unless `social/limits.md` sets another
time. After each round, compare what is left of each target with the hours left. When behind, work that platform in more
rounds per hour, up to its hourly ceilings and never above them, and never with waits under 10 seconds. When ahead, slow
down rather than finish early in a burst.
