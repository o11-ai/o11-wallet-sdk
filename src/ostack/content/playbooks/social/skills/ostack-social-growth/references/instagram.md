# Instagram

## Already commented

The person's comments: `https://www.instagram.com/your_activity/interactions/comments`. Note the posts from the last two
weeks already commented on.

## Find posts

Hashtag pages: `https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/<tag>/` with the tags in `social/niche.md`. Most posts are reels,
and the caption rarely says what the video is about.

## Understand the post before commenting

Let the reel play and look at the start, middle and end. Read the on-screen text and any captions burned into the video.
For carousels, go through every slide. If you still cannot tell what it says, like it and move on. Never comment from
the caption alone.

## Follow

Follow creators who post about the niche. Check their grid: most recent posts should be in the niche. Skip lifestyle,
crypto, "get rich" and course-funnel accounts.

## Limits

On "Try again later", "We restrict certain activity", or an action that does not stick after a refresh, stop Instagram
for the day.
