# X

## Already commented

The person's replies: `https://x.com/<handle>/with_replies`. Scroll the last few days and note the posts already
replied to.

## Find posts

- Search `https://x.com/search?q=<terms>&f=top` for strong posts and `&f=live` for fresh ones. Combine terms with `OR`
  and add `min_faves:20 since:<yesterday's date>`, for example `(outbound OR "cold email") min_faves:20 since:2026-11-14`.
- Once the best posts are used up, search `&f=live` with `min_faves:3` or no like filter for close posts: smaller
  accounts posting real takes in the niche.
- Open the post to read the full text or thread when it is cut off.
- Check the replies on strong niche posts for more niche people to follow.

## Follow

Follow authors of best and close niche posts and the good niche repliers under them. Open the profile first; follow
only if the bio or recent posts are in or next to the niche.

## Comment and repost

Reply to the freshest best posts first; early replies on big accounts get seen most. Repost the best few posts of the
day; never repost a close post. Quote one only when there is a short take worth adding, written with the same comment
rules.

## Limits

X shows "rate limited", "something went wrong, try again", or quietly undoes follows when you go too fast. On any of
these, stop X for the day.
