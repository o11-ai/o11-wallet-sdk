# X

## Already commented

The person's replies: `https://x.com/<handle>/with_replies`. Scroll the last few days and note the posts already
replied to.

## Find posts

- Search `https://x.com/search?q=<terms>&f=top` for strong posts and `&f=live` for fresh ones. Combine terms with `OR`
  and add `min_faves:20 since:<yesterday's date>`, for example `(outbound OR "cold email") min_faves:20 since:2026-11-14`.
- Open the post to read the full text or thread when it is cut off.
- Check the replies on strong niche posts for more niche people to follow.

## Follow

Follow authors of good niche posts and the best niche repliers under them. Open the profile first; follow only if the
bio or recent posts are in the niche.

## Comment and repost

Reply to the freshest strong posts first; early replies on big accounts get seen most. Repost the best few posts of the
day. Quote one only when there is a short take worth adding, written with the same comment rules.

## Limits

X shows "rate limited", "something went wrong, try again", or quietly undoes follows when you go too fast. On any of
these, stop X for the day.
