# Captions

Defaults for each platform. The row's prompt wins for that post, then the team's `social/captions.md`, then this file.

## Before writing

1. Know the one thing the post is about: the row's prompt, and for a video what it actually says and shows (read its
   script or watch the final cut).
2. Find the real material: the numbers, names and lines the row, the video or the team's files give you. Nothing else.
3. Write each platform from scratch for how people read there. Never paste one caption across platforms and trim it.

## X

- One punchy line, usually under 200 characters, that would make someone stop scrolling. Lead with the claim or the
  number, not the company name.
- No hashtags, at most one emoji, no "excited to announce". A link goes at the end only when the row gives one.
- A thread only when the prompt asks for one: the first post stands alone, then 3 to 6 short posts, one idea each.

## LinkedIn

- A first line that earns the click on "see more": the problem, the result or the surprising fact, under 15 words.
- Then 3 to 6 short paragraphs of one or two sentences: what changed, why it matters to the reader, one concrete detail.
- End with one clear next step or one real question. At most three hashtags, at the end.
- A link goes in a separate first comment, drafted beside the caption, unless the team's file says otherwise.

## Instagram

- The hook in the first line, since only it shows under the video. Then one to three short lines.
- One call to action that works without a link ("link in bio" only when the bio holds it).
- 3 to 8 specific hashtags on their own last line. Never banned or giant generic tags.
- Alt text for the video: one sentence on what is shown.

## Any other platform the row names

Follow how the best posts there read: TikTok and Shorts like Instagram but shorter; Threads and Bluesky like X.

## Extras

Draft only what the row or `social/captions.md` asks for: a thread, a first comment, alt text, a pinned reply, an
on-screen title. Label each so the team knows where it goes.

## Never

- Em dashes, "game changer", "excited to share", "we're thrilled", "revolutionize", "unlock", "in today's world".
- Claims the video or the row cannot back up, or tags of people and companies the row does not name.
