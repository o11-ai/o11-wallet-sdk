Read every official account the target links from its own site or bio: X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit for a company, plus Threads and a newsletter for a creator. Collect up to 60 recent posts per channel and go back no further than six months.

Rank posts inside one channel only. Compare the top quarter with the bottom half, look at the real thumbnails and covers, and write the pattern as counted traits. A channel with fewer than 20 settled posts gets no pattern.
