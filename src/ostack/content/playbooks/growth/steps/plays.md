Full mode ends in ten plays and creator mode in five. Order them by evidence first, then timing, lift and fit. Good timing means the move began before the inflection, and fit means it suits the user's stage. Each play states its evidence, effort, time to first result, cost to try and a prompt the user can paste into their agent.

Also list two or three things to skip, with the reason, and a 14-day plan for each channel the user should work. A play never asks the user to copy another company's words, people, footage or brand; it copies the structure.
