Settle the mode, the target (a domain, or a handle and its platform in creator mode), the time window (24 months by default; videos mode reads back to each account's first post), the user's company and stage (before launch, launched, first customers or early revenue, scaling up, or category leader), the spending cap and the country. Take whatever the request and the team's `company.md` already say and ask only for what is missing.

The cap defaults to $3. It is a ceiling for the whole teardown. Raising it takes the user's word, never the agent's judgment. The country defaults to the United States.
