Follow the growth skill's stages reference. Cut the company's history into stages at dated events (the launch, the inflection month in the traffic estimate, the first round labelled Series A or later, the next round of $100M or more), read every short video back to the first post, and describe each stage by its rhythm, formats, outliers, hooks and sounds. Judge outliers inside one platform, keep videos with a paid flag and creators' co-posts apart from the company's own, and say when hooks rest on captions. Place the user in the nearest stage from their own numbers, and write `ugc-handoff.json` with that stage's hook formulas, formats, sounds and reference links for the video skills. Never copy a later stage's playbook onto an earlier-stage company.
