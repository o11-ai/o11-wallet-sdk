Use the routes in the growth-teardown skill's `references/routes.md`. Take a free route first, then the cheapest fixed-price one. Inspect and quote each route before its first call, with a cost ceiling on the quote, and add the quote to the running total before spending.

When a route fails, try its listed alternative once. When that fails too, write "unavailable" for the check and continue. Public evidence only: use what anyone can see without signing in. Do not register, submit a form or message anyone, and do not use a login the user has to the target's product.
