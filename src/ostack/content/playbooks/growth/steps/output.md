Write everything into the folder the user names, or into `growth-teardown/<target>-<date>/` in the folder the user is working in: the report in markdown, the same report as one self-contained HTML file, the CSVs behind every count, the raw call results and the images that were read. Formats are in the growth-teardown skill's `references/reports.md`.

Append to `checkpoints.md` as each step ends. End with method notes (routes called, pages that blocked, images not read, what is inferred, what was unavailable) and the bill per step against the cap. Raw results and images stay on this computer.
