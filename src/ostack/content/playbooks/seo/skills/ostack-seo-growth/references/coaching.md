# Coaching the team's search growth

Point out what is holding their traffic back while you work and in every report, without being asked. How to say it, and what you may change yourself versus what needs their yes, is in the ostack-setup skill's `references/first-run.md` ("Coach without being asked"). The standard behind every point is the `seo/write` and `seo/topics` step defaults (`o11 ostack context seo/write`).

## Red flags on their site and pages

Name at most three, worst first, each with the page, why it costs traffic and the fix.

1. **Pages search cannot use.** Not indexed, blocked by robots or `noindex`, a canonical pointing elsewhere, or a slow or broken page. Nothing else matters until these are fixed.
2. **Titles that hide the search.** Over 60 characters, the brand first, or no words a buyer would type. The title is most of the click.
3. **Writing for the wrong searches.** Only top-of-funnel posts while "<competitor> alternative", "<you> vs <competitor>" and "best <category> for <audience>" have no page.
4. **Thin or generic pages.** Shorter and vaguer than the top results, no real numbers, steps or screenshots, or a rewrite of what already ranks.
5. **No answer up top.** The first two sentences do not answer the search, so AI answers and featured snippets quote someone else.
6. **Dead ends.** No links to related pages or the product page, or no call to action at the end.
7. **Slipping pages left alone.** Pages that fell from the top 10, or rank 4 to 20, without a refresh planned.
8. **Claims they cannot back.** Customers, numbers or "best" claims with no source; they hurt trust and AI answers skip them.

A site with none of these: say it is in good shape in one line and keep publishing.

## Their pages versus yours

- Pieces and refreshes you write: fix the flags before they ship, and say so in one line.
- Pages the team wrote: show the better title, opening or outline next to theirs and ask for a one-word yes. Their published pages change only through the team's normal publishing path (a pull request or CMS draft), never live.

## After results come in

Each tracking report, and each growth run that reads it, ends with a short "What to fix next" note from their own numbers (Search Console through their own connected Google account, rankings and AI answers):

- A page with impressions but few clicks: rewrite its title and description.
- A page ranking 4 to 20: refresh it before writing something new.
- A competitor or AI answer winning a search they have a page for: say what that page has that theirs lacks.
- Too little data yet (new site or under four weeks): say so with the counts so far.
