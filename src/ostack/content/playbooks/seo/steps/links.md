Links come from being worth citing, never from buying them or trading in schemes. Find the sites that already link to two
or more competitors and not to the team (backlink gaps), the pages AI assistants cite for the team's priority searches,
directories and review sites buyers in this market actually use, partners and integrations that list their partners,
and posts that mention the team without linking.

List each with the page, why it would link, and who to ask. When the gtm playbook is set up, hand the list to
ostack-outbound as an audience (a short `gtm/audiences/links/` with the pitch: the page of theirs it improves and the
page of ours it would cite); otherwise put it in the report for the team. Never send outreach from this playbook.
