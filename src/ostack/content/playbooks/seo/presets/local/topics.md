For a local business, searches carry a place: "<service> in <city>", "<service> near me", "best <business type> <city>".
Start with one page per service and one per area actually served (never a page for a town the business does not serve),
then answers to the questions customers ask before booking ("how much does <service> cost in <city>"). Check the map
pack with `o11 call o11.google.serp.local` and reviews with `o11 call o11.google.maps.reviews` for the business and the
top three competitors.
