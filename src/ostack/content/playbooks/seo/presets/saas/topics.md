For software sold to businesses, the first ten pieces are almost always: "<competitor> alternatives" for the two or three
competitors buyers know best, "<you> vs <competitor>" for each, "best <category> for <audience or company size>", and
the three jobs buyers search for before they know the category ("how to <job>", "<job> template", "<job> checklist").
Integration pages ("<you> + <tool they already use>") and use-case pages come next. Write the first ten from the team's
own knowledge of the market, with the founder's input, before scaling anything.
